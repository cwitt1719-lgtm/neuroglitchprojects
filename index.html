Hey, I'm Calin Witt.

Husband, dad, former cop, logistics guy, and AuDHD app builder.

I'm a member of the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma and husband to Erica. I'm dad to King, Legion, and KJ, plus three bonus kids. My three are all neurodivergent, and watching them struggle with how the world works is a big part of why Neuro-Glitch exists.

I didn't find out I was AuDHD until my 20s. Nobody around me was surprised except me. I've always been the guy who finds a problem and solves it, sometimes even making a problem just to solve it when I was bored.

Family

Family is my why. I build these apps for my own brain and my kids' future. When I'm not building, I'm probably throwing cornhole, sometimes in tournaments and always against my boys.

Law Enforcement

Law enforcement taught me about people: how to read them, how to stay calm when things go wrong, and how to actually help when someone's having their worst day.

Logistics

As a Dockmaster, I built spreadsheets to keep track of every truck coming in and going out. Turns out that's exactly how my brain works best: build a system once and let it do the remembering. That's every app I make.

I build apps that help my own brain, and then I share them so nobody has to struggle without the apps they need.
