Hey, I'm Calin Witt.

Husband, dad, former cop, logistics guy, and AuDHD app builder.

I'm a member of the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma and husband to Erica. I'm dad to King, Legion, and KJ, plus three bonus kids. My three are all neurodivergent, and watching them struggle with how the world works is a big part of why Neuro-Glitch exists.

I didn't find out I was AuDHD until my 20s. Nobody around me was surprised except me. I've always been the guy who finds a problem and solves it, sometimes even making a problem just to solve it when I was bored.

Family

Family is my why. I build these apps for my own brain and my kids' future.

Law Enforcement

Law enforcement taught me to stay calm when things go wrong and to have a plan when other people need help fast. That's where Neuro-Binder came from.

Logistics

As a Dockmaster, I built spreadsheets to keep track of every truck coming in and going out. Turns out that's exactly how my brain works best: build a system once and let it do the remembering. That's every app I make.

I build apps that help my own brain, and then I share them so nobody has to struggle without the tools they need.

My name is Calin Witt and I am the creator of Neuro-Glitch. I was born at the Cherokee Nation Hastings Hospital in Tahlequah, OK. Fun fact, I was the only baby born in the hospital that day if you believe my Mother! My dad (Mike) and my brother (Colton) taught me most of what I know and my little sister (Hailey) is one of my biggest inspirations (and my favorite sibling). I also have two other little brothers, who are not so little (Seth and Jace) and my Mother (Sandra), whom have all helped shape who I am. I am a member of the Choctaw Nation Tribe of Oklahoma. My family includes 6 total kids (3 mine and 3 bonus). My 3, King, Legion, and KJ are all Neuro-Divergent and all 3 have unique personalities (and 4 names each). They struggle with how the world is and I want to be a part of something that creates a better future for them! My wife (Erica) and I also have 2 beautiful dogs, Beau (long-haired dachshund) and Moose (my giant deaf Great Dane) and a Burmese Python (Kaa). Yes, from the Jungle Book!

Growing up I always knew I was a little different, I just never knew why. I have always been very good at problem-solving (sometimes creating problems just to solve them when I was bored). I discovered in my 20s I was AuDHD, to which no one around me was surprised….but me. I created Neuro-Glitch because I want to be able to help people with cognitive struggles by creating apps that I know help me. Finding a problem and solving it. I am a prior Law-Enforcement Officer as well. I never want anyone to need a tool and not have it so if you are really struggling and need help and one of my tools could help you but you can’t afford it, just let me know and we will work together to figure something out! Thank you all for visiting my site and I am so excited for this journey!

Questions

How do I get my app after I pay?

Right after Stripe checkout, an email shows up with your guide PDF and your app link. Usually within a few minutes.

What if the email doesn't show up?

Check spam and promotions first. Still nothing? Email me at help@neuroglitchprojects.com and I'll send it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. These are web apps. Open the link in your browser and tap “Add to Home Screen.” Works on phone, tablet, or computer.

Do I need an account?

No. Checkout is by Stripe. No sign-up here.

Are these monthly fees?

No. One-time purchase. No subscriptions sneaking up on you.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds, because these are web apps and there's no way to return them once you have them. But if something isn't working, email me and I'll gladly fix it.

I'm not autistic or ADHD. Can I still use these?

Yes. They're built for neurodivergent brains, but they work for anyone who forgets stuff. So, everyone.

Is Neuro-Binder really free?

Yes, free. Responders scan a QR code, no account needed. Editing the card needs a parent sign-in. It does not call 911.

Can you build a different app?

Yes. Use the custom app form above. I reply by email with an estimate.
